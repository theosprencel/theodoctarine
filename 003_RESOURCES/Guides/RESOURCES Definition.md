****~🔴R~ = ~🔴RESOURCES~** in the ***PARA Method: A Simple System for Organizing Your Digital Life***. Learn more at [**==🔴Forte Labs==**](https://fortelabs.com/blog/para/).

> Resources are "topics or interests that may be useful in the future." This is the most oddly defined of the four, in my opinion. I see what it is intended, but I’ve always found it easier to think of Resources as materials that help you with some type of work.
