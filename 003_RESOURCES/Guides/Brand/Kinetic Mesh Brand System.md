## What this document is

This is a document created for collaborative work between AI agents and me. Content includes:

- My "personal brand," the aesthetic of which has come to be named **Kinetic Mesh**.
  - What it means and where it came from
  - Color palette and themes
  - Typography guidelines and font specs
  - Cultivating a unique voice
  - Striking contextually appropriate, yet consistent, tone

Every effort is taken to provide information in accordance with what I know to be best practices, including those pertaining AI-readiness.

That said, I ain't perfect and I always welcome a nudge toward edification.

## What this document is NOT

This is not an official playbook or brand spec or anything with that level of sanctioning. The focus here is practicality: What will enable us to collaborate optimally and produce the best possible work?

Everything we do should contribute to answering that question and taking action accordingly. I'm even willing to work more in the terminal, so you know I'm serious about this 😁
