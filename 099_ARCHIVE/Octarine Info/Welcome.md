### **Welcome to Octarine :)**

Is this the 1857345th Note Editing app out there? Maybe? Would I be making promises, that this one is *different*? Haven't you heard that before?

[Octarine](https://octarine.app/) is a lot of things, `fundamentally new` is not one of them, and you know what - **That's fine**!

It does all the usual things that you'd expect from a note taker, but takes a few workflows that seemed to work with me, and gives it a try.

- **Private & Local-First** — Your notes live on your disk in plain Markdown. No servers, no vendor lock-in. If you ever feel like Octarine isn't the one (we hope you don't), you won't lose your notes.

- **Multiple Workspaces** — Separate work, personal, and side projects. Each workspace has its own settings, theme, and files. They're just folders on your disk!

- **Daily Desk & Weekly Notes** — Not everything needs to be a long thought-out note. Head over to Daily Desk to jot down what's on your mind for the day, and view them in a slick calendar. Weekly notes help you plan and reflect on a bigger scale.

- **Wikilinks & Bi-directional Linking** — Type `[[` to link to any note. Backlinks show up automatically so you can always see what links where.

- **Writing Assistant** — AI-powered chat that understands your notes. Ask questions, brainstorm, or get help writing.

- **Ask Octarine** — Chat with your entire workspace to find things, create notes or gain insights.

- **Properties** — Add structured metadata to any note.

- **Views** — Organize notes as Kanban boards or tables.

- **30+ Themes & Custom Theme Editor** — Pick a theme or design your own.

- **Templates & Slash Commands** — Type `/` to access quick actions and insert templates.

- **Git Sync & Backup** — Version control your notes and sync across devices.

**Keyboard shortcuts to remember**

- Press `CMD / Ctrl + K` to access the Command bar.
- Press `/` for slash commands
- Press `[[` to insert a wikilink
- Check all shortcuts from the `?` button on the bottom right

For more, visit the [docs](https://docs.octarine.app).
