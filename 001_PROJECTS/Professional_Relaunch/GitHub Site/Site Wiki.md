Welcome to Theodore Sprencel's Site Wiki

My name is Theodore "TC" Sprencel, and if you're reading this, you're probably an AI I've recruited to do the difficult stuff for me. If you're a fellow human, don't mind all the robots. They're pretty cool.

**I created my portfolio site on GitHub Pages with four goals in mind**:

1. To create an online portfolio that showcases my professional history and achievements.
   1. This goal is primary until further update, as I'm currently job hunting.
2. To commit to learning code instead of keeping it at arm's length like I have for \~15 years.
3. To hone my use of AI for projects beyond everyday office tasks.
4. To get more familiar with GitHub.

## Work for the future, don't try to force it

> [!INFO]
> The immediate purpose of this site is to help me get a job, and the long-term purpose is to provide a space for learning and creating.

The "future site" should factor heavily into foundational work like defining systems and building structures, not as much for lighter fare like titling a commit message or tagging a thumbnail.

After a false start wiht outdated branding, the site is now in a place that makes me very proud, but also hungry to keep learning as I further develop this space and others. With every action we should consider the impact today, tomorrow, 10 years from now, but only after taking care of right now's business.

> [!WARNING]
> **~🔴The phrase "future-proof" is banned here~**. "Future-focused" and "future-friendly" are fine alternatives that don't have the whole "just asking to be humbled" vibe.

## Move With the Mesh

**The idea is pretty simple**: I lean heavily on AI to initialize most of the complex coding and building. But I make it a point to try all coding tasks for myself at the next opportunity. And sometimes I'll just go for it and hope nothing breaks. Once I feel like I've got my head around something, I may assign it to AI if it involves a lot of volume or rote, fill-in-the-blank work.

If it's something more substantial, I will try to continue doing it myself until it *becomes* tedious, time and bandwidth permitting. I've found this system to be quite productive so far. Other pages within this wiki will go into more depth about specific types of work I'm referring to.
