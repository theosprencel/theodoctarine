## What is the Projects folder?

In keeping with the [PARA organization method](https://fortelabs.com/blog/para/), **Projects** here means work that has an end. The date may not be known, but as long as the nature of the initiative suggests it will have a definitive end date, then it meets the qualification.

Further, it must be more than a task. This basically just means that it consists of two or more units of work that are discrete, coherent and oriented toward a shared goal.

The Projects folder is for focused, well-defined efforts toward some specified outcome (i.e., “projects”). Usually, any note that can go in the Projects folder should be categorizable into an appropriate child folder, as it should be part of a specific initiative.

## What should and should NOT go in the Projects folder?

| **Material** | **Should it go in the Projects folder?** | **Comments** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notes and attachments related to a website redesign | Yes |  |
| A couple quick notes reminding to correct misapplied brand colors | No | They're about the same subject, but they don't combine to work toward a specific outcome. |
| Established documentation that should be collected in one place | No | This is just an impetus to stay organized, not a specific goal that's being worked on. |

When a **Project** is done, its materials will often warrant relocating to one of the following:

- **Reference** folder: For materials that need to be referenced indefinitely
- **Archive** folder: For materials that could be useful at some point, but no immediate need is seen to make them referenceable.
- **Trash**: For materials that will definitely serve no purpose beyond the project.

For materials filed to **Documentation** or **Archive**, it will usually make sense to add a folder for the project, or a folder to contain materials from similar projects.
